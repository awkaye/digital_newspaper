EDITORIAL

Youth Participation Can Transform Our Community

Young people play an important role in improving Marikina City. Students and teenagers bring fresh ideas, energy, and creativity that can help solve local problems. When youth participate in community activities, they become more responsible and aware of civic duties.

Many issues such as flood management, waste reduction, environmental protection, and volunteer initiatives benefit from youth involvement. Schools and local governments should provide programs that allow students to share ideas and take part in community projects. Youth organizations can help organize activities that strengthen the city and foster unity. By working together, young people and adults can create a cleaner, safer, and more progressive Marikina.
