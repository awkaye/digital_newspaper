Free Medical and Dental Mission Serves Marikina Residents

City health program provides consultation, dental care, and free medicines

The Marikina City government recently conducted a free medical and dental mission to provide health services to residents. The program was held on March 14 at Malanday Elementary School and on March 15 at Fortune High School. The second day of the event focused on women in celebration of National Women’s Month. Residents were able to receive medical consultations, dental services, and free medicines from volunteer doctors and health workers.

The activity is part of the city’s ongoing effort to expand healthcare services for families in Marikina. According to Marjorie Ann 'Maan' Teodoro, accessible healthcare programs help ensure that every family in the community stays healthy and protected. Many residents expressed gratitude for the free services, especially those who cannot easily afford medical checkups.

Marikina Bike Lane Project Reaches 67% Completion

New cycling network aims to promote safer and sustainable transportation

Construction of new bike lanes in Marikina City is now 67 percent complete, according to updates from the Department of Transportation. The project will create an eight-kilometer cycling network connecting several major roads, including Sumulong Avenue, J.P. Rizal Street, and A. Bonifacio Avenue. The bike lanes are designed to provide safer routes for cyclists and encourage more residents to use bicycles as a form of transportation.

Officials explained that the project supports efforts to make roads safer and more accessible for everyone. The initiative also promotes environmentally friendly transportation options. Once completed, the bike lane network is expected to improve mobility and reduce traffic congestion in the city.

Inter-Agency Task Force Improves City Infrastructure

Daily operations repair roads, sidewalks, and public spaces

An Inter-Agency Task Force continues to conduct daily operations across different parts of Marikina City to improve public infrastructure. The teams perform various tasks such as tree cutting, wire bundling, repainting of electric posts, and maintenance of sidewalks and roads. They also repaint walls that were previously vandalized to keep the city clean and visually appealing.

The program is part of the city government’s efforts to maintain safe and orderly communities. Officials also educate residents about the importance of caring for public spaces and protecting the environment. Through cooperation between the government and citizens, Marikina aims to keep its neighborhoods clean and well-maintained.

Pasig–Marikina River Improvement Project Inspected

Officials review progress of flood control project

Government officials recently inspected the ongoing Pasig–Marikina River Channel Improvement Project to monitor its progress. The inspection was led by Vince Dizon together with city leaders. The project aims to strengthen flood control measures and improve the flow of water along the river.

Officials emphasized that improving the river channel is important in preventing severe flooding during heavy rains. The project is expected to benefit thousands of residents living near the riverbanks. Leaders expressed hope that the improvements will help protect communities and enhance disaster preparedness in the city.



Car-Free Sundays Encourage Healthy Lifestyle

Residents enjoy safer streets for walking and cycling

Marikina City continues to promote healthier lifestyles through its Car-Free Sunday program. During this activity, certain roads are temporarily closed to vehicles so residents can walk, jog, and cycle freely. The program provides a safe and enjoyable space for families, children, and fitness enthusiasts.

According to city officials, the initiative also supports the city’s goal of creating inclusive and safe public spaces. The activity encourages residents to stay active while strengthening the sense of community. Many families participate regularly, turning the event into a weekly tradition for recreation and exercise.
