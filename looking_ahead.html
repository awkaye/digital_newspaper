FUTURE OF OUR COMMUNITY

Our Community in 2035

By 2035, Marikina City could transform into a modern, connected, and sustainable community that balances technology, environment, and citizen well-being. Schools may feature smart classrooms with interactive screens, digital textbooks, and online learning platforms, making education more engaging and accessible. Students could learn at their own pace while teachers track progress in real time, allowing for personalized instruction. Community learning centers may also provide workshops and training for adults and youth, ensuring opportunities for lifelong learning.

Transportation across the city may become safer, faster, and more eco-friendly. Modern public transport, expanded bike lanes, and well-organized traffic systems could make commuting easier for workers and students. Electric or autonomous vehicles may reduce pollution, while pedestrian-friendly streets encourage walking and cycling. These improvements, combined with real-time traffic monitoring and smart traffic lights, would create a more efficient and sustainable urban environment.

In addition, Marikina may strengthen youth programs, digital government services, and environmental initiatives. Leadership workshops, cultural events, and sports activities could engage young residents in shaping their community, while digital platforms make it easier to access government services online. Reforestation, river rehabilitation, and eco-friendly projects may protect the city from climate risks and enhance its green spaces. By 2035, Marikina City could become an innovative, inclusive, and resilient community, where technology, responsible governance, and active citizen participation work together to improve the quality of life for all residents.
