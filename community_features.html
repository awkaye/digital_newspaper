COMMUNITY FEATURES

Top 5 Community Issues:

  1. Flood Management and Control
    Marikina City faces frequent flooding, especially during heavy rains, due to its location in a valley and along the Marikina River. The city has implemented flood control measures, but overflow during storms still affects residents and infrastructure.
  2. Urban Poor Displacement and Housing
    Informal settler families along riverbanks face displacement due to safety concerns and demolitions. Adequate relocation within the city remains a pressing challenge for affected residents.
  3. Solid Waste Management and Pollution
    Trash and plastic waste left in waterways after heavy rains continue to be a problem. The city organizes clean-up drives, but proper waste management by residents is essential.
  4. Traffic Management and Infrastructure
    Traffic congestion remains a concern during rush hours, particularly on major roads like Sumulong Highway. Ongoing road and drainage projects sometimes disrupt daily commuting.
  5. Environmental Vulnerability to Climate Change
    Barangays such as Tumana, Nangka, and Malanday are highly exposed to typhoons and rising floodwaters. The city continues efforts in disaster risk reduction and environmental rehabilitation.

Did you know?

Local Statistic:

Marikina City continues to grow, with thousands of students enrolled in public schools every year.

Historical Fact:

Marikina is known as the “Shoe Capital of the Philippines” and has a long history of shoemaking dating back to the Spanish colonial era.

Fun Fact:

Marikina regularly holds environmental programs, including recycling drives, tree planting activities, and city clean-up projects.
